# Welcome Email Sequence

## Noura Botanics – Email Marketing Campaign

### Overview

The welcome email sequence is designed for people who have recently subscribed to Noura Botanics. Its purpose is to introduce the brand, share useful skincare information, and help subscribers discover products that may interest them.

The sequence will include three emails sent over four days. Each email will have a different purpose so subscribers are introduced to the brand gradually rather than receiving too much promotional content at once.

### Email 1: Welcome to Noura Botanics

**Timing:** Immediately after sign-up

**Purpose:** Introduce the brand and make a positive first impression.

**Main content:**
- Welcome the subscriber to Noura Botanics.
- Briefly explain the brand's approach to everyday skincare.
- Introduce the idea that skincare can be simple and affordable.
- Encourage subscribers to explore the brand.

**Main call to action:** Explore the Collection

### Email 2: Skincare Made Simple

**Timing:** Two days after sign-up

**Purpose:** Provide useful information and introduce the brand's approach to simple skincare routines.

**Main content:**
- Explain the importance of keeping a skincare routine simple.
- Introduce basic product categories such as cleansers and moisturisers.
- Explain product purposes in clear, easy-to-understand language.
- Help subscribers understand what they might need in a routine.

**Main call to action:** Discover Your Essentials

### Email 3: Find Your Everyday Essentials

**Timing:** Four days after sign-up

**Purpose:** Encourage product discovery by showing subscribers what Noura Botanics offers.

**Main content:**
- Introduce selected products from the collection.
- Explain what each product is intended for.
- Highlight useful product information.
- Encourage subscribers to explore products that match their interests.

**Main call to action:** Shop the Collection

### Sequence Summary

| Email | Timing | Main Objective |
|---|---|---|
| Email 1 | Immediately | Introduce the brand |
| Email 2 | Day 2 | Educate subscribers |
| Email 3 | Day 4 | Encourage product discovery |

### Overall Approach
The sequence will focus on building familiarity and trust before pushing for a purchase. Each email will have one main message and a clear call to action.

The tone will remain friendly, straightforward, and informative, in line with Noura Botanics' brand positioning.
