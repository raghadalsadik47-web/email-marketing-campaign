**Preview text:** A few basics to help you build your everyday routine.

Hi {{first_name}},

There's no shortage of skincare advice out there. In fact, there's probably more than anyone asked for.

If you're putting together an everyday routine, you don't need to start with a shelf full of products. Let's go back to basics.

**1. Cleanse**

A cleanser helps wash away dirt, oil and the buildup from the day. It's a simple first step that gets your skin ready for the next steps in your routine.

**2. Moisturize**

Moisturizer helps keep your skin hydrated and comfortable. Look for one that feels good on your skin.

**3. Protect**

Sunscreen helps protect your skin from UV damage, so it's worth making part of your daytime routine.

That's a good place to start. You can always adjust as you learn what works for your skin, and there's no need to buy everything at once.
