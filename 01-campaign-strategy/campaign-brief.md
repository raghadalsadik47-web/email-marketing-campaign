
# Noura Botanics
## Email Marketing Campaign Brief

### 1. Brand Overview

Noura Botanics is a fictional skincare and body-care brand created for this portfolio project.

The brand offers simple, affordable skincare products and aims to make everyday skincare feel approachable and easy to understand.

### 2. Campaign Overview

**Campaign name:** Welcome to Noura Botanics

**Campaign type:** Email marketing — Welcome Series

**Channel:** Email

**Campaign format:** A three-email automated welcome sequence followed by a promotional newsletter.

### 3. Campaign Purpose

The purpose of this campaign is to introduce new subscribers to Noura Botanics, communicate the brand's identity, build trust, and encourage potential customers to explore its product range.

### 4. Campaign Objectives

- Introduce new subscribers to the brand.
- Communicate the brand's values and product offering.
- Build familiarity and trust through useful skincare content.
- Encourage subscribers to explore the product range.
- Develop a consistent email communication strategy.

### 5. Target Audience

The initial target audience is skincare-interested consumers who want affordable, straightforward products and accessible skincare information.

The audience profile will be refined through further research in the audience research section.

### 6. Campaign Deliverables

1. Three-email welcome sequence.
2. Promotional newsletter.
3. Email design mockups.
4. Audience segmentation strategy.
5. Email marketing calendar.
6. KPI measurement framework.

### 7. Measurement Approach

The campaign will use the following metrics to evaluate its hypothetical performance:

- Email delivery rate.
- Open rate.
- Click-through rate.
- Conversion rate.
- Unsubscribe rate.

No actual campaign results will be reported because this project will not be deployed.

### 8. Project Disclaimer

Noura Botanics is a fictional brand. This campaign is a self-directed educational portfolio project and does not represent work completed for a real company.
