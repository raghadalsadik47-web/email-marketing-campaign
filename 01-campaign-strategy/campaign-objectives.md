
# Noura Botanics
## Email Marketing Campaign Objectives

### 1. Primary Campaign Goal

Introduce new subscribers to Noura Botanics, build familiarity with the brand, and encourage them to explore its everyday skincare and body-care products.

### 2. Marketing Objectives

**Objective 1: Build Brand Awareness**

Introduce subscribers to the brand's philosophy of straightforward, accessible skincare.

**Objective 2: Educate Subscribers**

Communicate the purpose of the products, their ingredients, and how they fit into simple everyday routines.

**Objective 3: Encourage Product Discovery**

Direct subscribers to relevant product pages through clear calls to action.

**Objective 4: Build Subscriber Engagement**

Develop a consistent and useful email experience that encourages subscribers to interact with the brand.

### 3. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| Objective | KPI |
|---|---|
| Brand awareness | Email open rate |
| Subscriber engagement | Click-through rate |
| Product discovery | Product page visits |
| Purchase interest | Conversion rate |
| List health | Unsubscribe rate |

### 4. Hypothetical Measurement Targets

The following figures are proposed planning targets for this fictional campaign. They are not industry benchmarks or actual results.

| KPI | Proposed target |
|---|---:|
| Email delivery rate | 98% |
| Unique open rate | 35% |
| Click-through rate | 3% |
| Unsubscribe rate | Below 0.5% |

Conversion rate will also be monitored, but a numerical target will be established once the hypothetical website and checkout journey are defined.

### 5. Measurement Notes

Campaign performance would be reviewed using email marketing analytics.

Results would be assessed across the welcome sequence to identify which messages generate the strongest engagement and product interest.

### 6. Project Disclaimer

These objectives and targets are hypothetical planning assumptions for an educational portfolio project. No real campaign has been deployed and no actual performance data has been collected.
