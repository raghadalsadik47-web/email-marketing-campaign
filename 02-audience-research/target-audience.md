# Target Audience Research

## Noura Botanics – Email Marketing Campaign

### 1. Target Market

Noura Botanics is a fictional skincare brand intended for an international audience. Its marketing communication will be developed in English, with a focus on customers in markets such as the United States, United Kingdom, and United Arab Emirates.

The campaign will target people who are interested in skincare and prefer products that are affordable, easy to understand, and suitable for everyday use.

For this project, the selected markets represent the brand's proposed audience rather than confirmed sales or distribution locations.

### 2. Primary Target Audience

The primary audience will consist of young adults between the ages of 20 and 35 who are interested in building or maintaining a simple skincare routine.

They may already use skincare products or be looking to improve their current routine. They are likely to look at product ingredients, read reviews, and compare prices before making a purchase.

The campaign will focus on customers who want to take care of their skin without feeling that they need an expensive or complicated routine.

**Audience characteristics:**

- **Age:** 20–35.
- **Location:** English-speaking international markets, particularly the US, UK, and UAE.
- **Interests:** Skincare, beauty, personal care, and ingredient information.
- **Shopping habits:** Research products online, compare prices, and read product reviews.
- **Main preference:** Affordable products with clear descriptions and easy-to-understand benefits.

These characteristics are proposed assumptions for the campaign and would need to be tested against actual customer research if the brand were real.

### 3. Audience Needs and Interests

Based on Noura Botanics' proposed positioning, the campaign will focus on several needs that are likely to matter to its intended customers.

**Simple skincare routines**

Some customers may find skincare overwhelming because of the number of products and routines available. They may prefer a few everyday essentials rather than a complicated routine.

**Affordable products**

Price can influence purchasing decisions, particularly for customers who want to maintain a regular skincare routine without spending too much.

**Clear product information**

Customers may want to understand what a product does, which ingredients it contains, and how it fits into their routine before purchasing it.

**Ingredient awareness**

Customers interested in skincare may look for information about ingredients and their purpose. Clear explanations can help them make more informed purchasing decisions.

**Convenience**

Customers may prefer being able to discover products, understand their uses, and shop online without spending too much time researching each item.

### 4. Potential Customer Challenges

Noura Botanics' communication should consider some of the difficulties customers may experience when choosing skincare products.

- Too many skincare products and routines can make it difficult to decide where to start.
- Some product descriptions use technical language that customers may not understand.
- Higher-priced products may not fit everyone's budget.
- Customers may be unsure which products are relevant to their needs.
- Promotional emails that focus only on discounts may not give customers enough reason to engage with a brand.

These challenges provide opportunities for Noura Botanics to make its communication more useful and straightforward.

### 5. What This Means for Email Marketing

The campaign should address these needs through clear, helpful, and easy-to-read emails.

The emails could:

- Introduce the brand and explain what makes it different.
- Share simple information about product ingredients.
- Explain product benefits without making exaggerated claims.
- Recommend products based on everyday skincare needs.
- Introduce promotions without making every email feel like an advertisement.

The overall aim is to make subscribers more familiar with the brand and its products while giving them useful reasons to keep reading its emails.

### 6. Summary

Noura Botanics' proposed target audience consists of young adults interested in affordable and straightforward skincare.

The campaign will focus on three main priorities: keeping skincare simple, making product information easy to understand, and helping customers discover products that suit their everyday routines.

This audience profile will be used as a starting point for developing the customer persona and planning the email campaign.
