# Customer Persona

## Noura Botanics – Email Marketing Campaign

### 1. Persona Overview

**Name:** Olivia Bennett  
**Age:** 27  
**Location:** London, United Kingdom  
**Occupation:** Marketing Assistant  
**Lifestyle:** Busy working professional who enjoys skincare and personal care.

Olivia represents the type of customer Noura Botanics hopes to attract. She is interested in looking after her skin but prefers a routine that is simple, affordable, and easy to maintain.

*Note: Olivia is a fictional persona created for this campaign. She does not represent an actual customer.*

### 2. Skincare Habits

Olivia has a basic skincare routine and usually looks for products that are easy to use and fit into her daily schedule.

She is interested in learning about skincare ingredients but does not want to spend too much time researching every product. Before purchasing something new, she may check product descriptions, reviews, and prices.

She prefers products that serve a clear purpose rather than buying several products without knowing how they work together.

### 3. Goals and Motivations

- Maintain a simple and consistent skincare routine.
- Find products that fit her budget.
- Understand what she is applying to her skin.
- Discover products that are easy to incorporate into her everyday routine.
- Find brands that communicate honestly and clearly.

### 4. Challenges and Frustrations

- Complicated skincare routines can feel overwhelming.
- Some skincare brands use technical language that is difficult to understand.
- Premium skincare products can be expensive.
- It can be difficult to decide which products are worth purchasing.
- Repeated promotional emails can become annoying if they offer little useful information.

### 5. Shopping Behaviour

Olivia usually discovers skincare products through social media, online searches, and recommendations.

Before making a purchase, she may:

- Compare prices between different brands.
- Read product descriptions and customer reviews.
- Look at ingredient lists.
- Check whether a product suits her skincare needs.
- Wait for an offer before purchasing.

She is more likely to consider a brand if its products are clearly explained and its communication feels genuine.

### 6. Email Marketing Preferences

Olivia would be more interested in emails that provide useful information rather than only advertising products.

She may respond positively to:

- Welcome emails that introduce the brand and its products.
- Short explanations of skincare ingredients.
- Simple skincare tips and routine suggestions.
- Product recommendations that are relevant to her interests.
- Occasional discounts and special offers.

Emails should be visually appealing, easy to read on a phone, and short enough to hold her attention.

### 7. How Noura Botanics Can Reach Her

Noura Botanics should focus on clear product descriptions, accessible ingredient information, and recommendations that make skincare feel less complicated.

Its email campaign should introduce the brand gradually, provide useful information, and give Olivia reasons to explore its products without making every interaction feel like a sales pitch.

### 8. Key Insight

Olivia is not necessarily looking for the largest skincare collection or the most complicated routine. She wants products she understands, can afford, and can comfortably include in her everyday life.

Noura Botanics can use this insight to create emails that focus on simplicity, useful information, and accessible skincare.
