# Audience Segmentation

## Noura Botanics – Email Marketing Campaign

### Purpose

Audience segmentation means dividing email subscribers into smaller groups based on their interests or how they interact with the brand. This helps a business send emails that are more relevant instead of sending the same message to everyone.

For Noura Botanics, the segmentation plan will focus on subscriber activity and product interests. Since this is a proposed campaign for a fictional brand, the segments below are recommendations rather than existing customer groups.

### 1. New Subscribers

**Who are they?**

People who have recently signed up to receive emails from Noura Botanics.

**What might they need?**

- An introduction to the brand and what it offers.
- Information about the products and their purpose.
- A reason to explore the website.

**Email approach:**

Send a welcome email sequence introducing the brand, explaining its approach to everyday skincare, and helping subscribers discover its products.

### 2. Engaged Subscribers

**Who are they?**

Subscribers who regularly open emails, click on links, or visit product pages through email campaigns.

**What might they need?**

- More information about products they have shown interest in.
- Skincare tips and useful content.
- Updates about new products or promotions.

**Email approach:**

Share product information, helpful content, and occasional promotional emails. The aim is to maintain their interest without sending too many sales-focused messages.

### 3. Product-Interest Subscribers

**Who are they?**

Subscribers who have shown interest in particular products or product categories by clicking on email links or choosing their preferences.

**What might they need?**

- More details about products they have explored.
- Information about ingredients and how products are intended to be used.
- Recommendations related to their interests.

**Email approach:**

Send relevant product information and recommendations based on the categories they have shown interest in. For example, someone who frequently explores moisturisers could receive an email introducing the brand's moisturiser range.

### How the Segments Would Be Created

Noura Botanics could use a few simple methods to organise its subscribers:

- **Sign-up forms:** Collect email addresses and optionally allow subscribers to select product categories they are interested in.
- **Email activity:** Use clicks and other engagement data to identify subscribers who interact with campaigns.
- **Website activity:** Where consent and appropriate tracking are in place, use visits to product pages to understand product interests.

Subscribers should be able to manage their preferences and unsubscribe from marketing emails.

### How Segmentation Supports the Campaign

For the proposed welcome campaign, all new subscribers would receive the welcome sequence. As subscribers interact with the emails, they could gradually be grouped based on their activity and interests.

This would allow Noura Botanics to send more relevant product information and promotions over time, rather than treating every subscriber the same way.

### Conclusion

The segmentation plan focuses on three groups: new subscribers, engaged subscribers, and subscribers with specific product interests. Starting with these groups would give Noura Botanics a manageable way to organise its email campaigns while learning more about what its audience wants.
